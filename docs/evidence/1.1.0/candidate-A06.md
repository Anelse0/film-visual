# A06 | 1.1.0

## 原始请求

设计明亮而拥挤的小型地下舞厅，16:9，手绘动画，大量红、绿、紫色，想要喧闹和不整齐，多组人物同等重要，不要单个主角，也不要变成低饱和高级灰。只给一条英文图像 prompt。

## 实际输出（仅路径脱敏）

A bright, overcrowded small basement dance club, 16:9 wide composition, hand-drawn 2D animation with lively uneven ink contours, expressive poses, and opaque gouache textures. Viewed diagonally from the foot of the entrance stairs beneath a low ceiling crowded with exposed pipes and colored lamps; a packed dance floor squeezed between a makeshift DJ table and mismatched perimeter seating. Multiple equally important groups fill the frame: friends dancing in loose circles, people laughing over drinks, animated conversations beside stacked speakers, overlapping bodies and gestures extending beyond both edges. Distribute comparable detail, brightness, and color intensity across these groups, with no central protagonist or isolated spotlight. Abundant saturated scarlet, vivid green, and electric purple recur in broad wall sections, clashing clothes, painted furniture, and pools of colored light. Visible ceiling fixtures spread bright illumination throughout the cramped room; colored shadows remain luminous, with small local value changes keeping faces and limbs readable. Crooked posters, scattered cups, tangled cables, scuffed flooring, irregular spacing, exuberant visual noise and disorder. No muted grading, desaturated gray palette, cavernous darkness, or polished minimalist styling.
